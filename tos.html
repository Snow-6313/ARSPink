Additional Rules That Still Apply

The sections above set the tone. These are the operational rules that still apply to every layout request and profile change.

  • Keep the site free of Roblox-specific integration.
  • Keep the purple palette and the one-game focus intact.
  • Do not reintroduce external data feeds unless they are truly needed.
  • Keep the profile loader tied to the selected Discord ID.
  • Use support only for visual direction changes and content updates.
  • Keep motion noticeable but not excessive.
  • Maintain the split hero and the featured profile card as the focal point.
  • Keep the site responsive on desktop and mobile.
  • Respect the existing brand unless a full redesign is requested.
